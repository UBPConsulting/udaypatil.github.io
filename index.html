What I do

Closing the gap between project completion and operations.

Commissioning is not only about starting equipment. Operational readiness requires the right systems, procedures, maintenance strategy, resources and people to be in place at the same time.

Commissioning management

Mechanical completion, pre-commissioning, commissioning, system turnover and start-up coordination.

Operational readiness

Preparing people, processes, documentation, procedures and resources for transition to operations.

Maintenance readiness

Maintenance planning, asset hierarchy, PM strategy, spares, equipment readiness and work management.

Systemization & turnover

Systems, boundaries, punch lists, turnover documentation and readiness tracking.

Start-up & ramp-up

Hands-on support through start-up, troubleshooting, stabilization and transition to operations.

Reliability & improvement

RCA, FMEA, maintenance improvement, SOPs, CAPA and operational excellence initiatives.
